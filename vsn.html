You list your address. They list theirs. Email in between can then be checked against both listings. If they ignore the invitation, they can still use the free check — it costs them nothing and requires no account.

What you see

Three possible answers

Address listedThis address appears in that organization's listing.
Address not listedSignet7 has no listing covering this address. Very common, and not an accusation.
Address mismatchThe organization has a listing, but this address is not in it.

What this is not

It is not a public directory of companies

You cannot browse a list of every business. Hosted listings on the company account after they enroll, not a public directory — they exist only for organizations that have registered, and they are visible to the parties actually corresponding. Signet7 can also read a signing key an organization has published in its own DNS records, if it has chosen to do that.

The other organization installs nothing

If you use Signet7, they get the free check and an invitation. They only install Signet7 desktop if they decide they want to sign their own email.

If they use Signet7 too

Then signing and the listing both happen automatically when you send. That is the two-way link, and it is where the checking gets easy.

Nothing is added to your email

Signet7 does not insert a badge, footer, or check link into the message you write. Invitations come from Signet7 separately, once per recipient.
