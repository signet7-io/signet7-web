Crisis and Self-Harm Reply Policy · effective 19 August 2026
DRAFT — NON-OPERATIVE

If you are in crisis, get help now.

Effective date: 19 August 2026. This is the Signet7 Crisis and Self-Harm Reply Policy.

United States: if you are in crisis or thinking about suicide, call or text 988 now. 988lifeline.org

1. What Signet7 will do

If crisis language appears in a decision request, MCP tool call, or mailbox-agent preview, Signet7 holds the action. It does not issue a seal, policy allow, or verify result for that request. It returns the 988 resource instead.

2. What Signet7 will not do

  • Signet7 is not a counselor, clinician, or emergency service.
  • A crisis hold is not a medical diagnosis.
  • Verified is not safe. Unknown is not fraud.

3. How to get help

United States: 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. International: IASP resource finder.

Do not send a live crisis message into a public verify demo. Call 988. See Privacy Policy and AI use.
