Crisis and self-harm · 2026-08-19
DRAFT — NON-OPERATIVE

If you are in crisis, get help now.

Signet7 is not a counselor and not a chatbot. If crisis language appears in a decision, verify, or mailbox-agent path, Signet7 holds the action and points to help instead of issuing a seal or policy verdict.

United States: call or text 988, the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. 988lifeline.org

International resources: IASP resource finder.

Verified is not safe. Unknown is not fraud. A crisis hold is not a medical diagnosis.

Do not send a live crisis message into a public verify demo. Call 988.
