Companies do not sign a paper. Acceptance is an empty box on the account page.

A check tells you whether the protected words still match the seal, and whether that From address is listed. It does not tell you a payment is safe. It is not a court stamp. You upload a saved message so the check can compare the seal. Signet7 does not keep a copy of the file after the check. The check has to look at the file to do the comparison. We do not train a model on that file or on your mail. We do not sell the account record. We do not use it for advertising.

The Evidence Vault stays on the office computer, inside the Signet7 app. It is not an online vault. The app is not code-signed. Windows may say the publisher is unknown. The Mac may ask you to confirm.

This website does not set a tracking cookie or an advertising pixel. There is no cookie banner, because there is no non-essential cookie to reject. If a non-essential cookie is added later, nothing non-essential loads before a choice, and reject is as easy as accept.
