What Signet7 holds, what it does not, and for how long.

This page is the public desk for privacy, security, and retention. It matches the running product. It is not SOC 2, ISO, FedRAMP, a penetration-test report, or a formed-company privacy notice. Privacy and Terms stay marked draft until counsel signs them.

Privacy Security Terms

Privacy

What we collect

Public check

You submit a saved message so Signet7 can hash and check it in memory. Default public check does not keep the raw letter.

Company desk

Work email, company legal name you type, people you add, named work emails, listing keys you publish. No Signet7 password. We email a one-time link.

Desktop on your computer

The app reads named work emails on that computer. Status lines can go to the company desk. Message bodies are not the dashboard. Optional vault stays on that computer. You hold the recovery key.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use it for ads. We do not train a generative model on company mail. Live billing is off, so Stripe is not a live subprocessor. See Data providers and AI use.

Retention

How long it stays

Raw letters

Not retained by default. Transient uploads are deleted after the check. A deletion request purges those transients. It does not silently rewrite historical hash rows.

Evidence rows

Only if an authorized operator turns evidence on. Those rows are hashes, keys, and status — not the readable email.

Ask us to delete

Email justin.daines@signet7.io with the word Delete, or call POST /api/v1/privacy/deletion-request on an operated instance. Identity checks apply.

There is no seven-year default. US or EU record-keeping talk does not make Signet7 an archive. Counsel still decides what you must keep. Optional vault cap is 10 GB on that company computer.

Security

What is in the product today

Seals

Ed25519 signatures on supported mail. A pass means the protected words still match that key. It is not a blessing that the email is wise or safe. See what a result means.

Desktop download

Unsigned preview zips. Windows SmartScreen and Mac Gatekeeper will ask extra clicks. Code-signing is not done. The files are public; using named work emails still needs a company account.

What this is not

Not SOC 2. Not ISO 27001. Not FedRAMP. Not a penetration test. Not an SLA. Object stores are configured with public-access blocks. That is not a live-account audit.
