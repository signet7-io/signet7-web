Cancel and trial reminder · 2026-08-19
DRAFT — NON-OPERATIVE

Cancel is one request. There is no hidden trial.

United States law on internet negative-option sales requires clear terms before billing data is taken, express consent before a charge, and a simple way to stop recurring charges. Signet7 keeps those rules even while live billing is off.

How to cancel

  1. If a test or future paid program exists, open Account and choose Cancel access.
  2. That calls the first-party cancel route. A billing portal is not required.
  3. If no subscription exists, the server says so. You are not sent through extra pages.
  4. You may also email the published contact channel with the word Cancel.

Trials

The current catalog has zero trial days and does not auto-convert. A later trial cannot charge unless a reminder was sent at least twenty-four hours before the charge. Free recipient verification is not a trial.

No public page creates a live charge. offer_live remains false until a separate activation decision.
