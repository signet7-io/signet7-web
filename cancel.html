3. How to cancel

Cancel is one request. A billing portal is not required.

  1. Open Account and choose Cancel access, or
  2. Call POST /api/v1/billing/cancel on an operated instance, or
  3. Email justin.daines@signet7.io with the word Cancel.

This policy follows the ROSCA rule for internet negative-option sales: clear terms before billing data is taken, express consent before a charge, and a simple way to stop recurring charges. See Privacy Policy.
