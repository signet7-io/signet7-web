What the recipient does

Four steps. One result.

1. Keep the email

Save the original. A forward can drop what the check needs. A phone often cannot export it. Use a computer if you have to.

2. Open the check

Website: no account. Outlook: stay on the open message. You do not check junk — only the email that asks you to move money or change an account. Verified is not safe. Unknown is not fraud.

3. Verifiable Sender Network (VSN)

The Verifiable Sender Network (VSN) looks up whether the signing key is still bound to that organization at verify.signet7.io/vsn. Today only Signet7's own domain is in that lookup.

4. Read the result

Matched, unmatched, or unknown. Then you decide. Verified does not mean safe. Unknown does not mean fraud.
