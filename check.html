What you do

Four steps.

1. Keep the email

Save the original. A forward can drop what the check needs. A phone often cannot export it. Use a computer if you have to.

2. Open the live check

Website: no account. Outlook can check the open message if the pane is already there. You do not check junk. You check the email that asks you to move money or change an account.

3. Read the words

Match, do not match, or no seal. No seal is ordinary mail. Checking does not create a seal.

4. Read the listing

Listed for this address, not listed, or listing does not match. Call a number you already have.
