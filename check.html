Under 60 seconds

Check the email that asks you to act.

Save the email, upload it, and read the answer. That is the whole process — no account, no login, and nothing to install. Recipients are not required to install anything in order to check an email you sent them.

If the email arrived with a Signet7-sealed.eml attachment, upload that attachment. If it did not, upload the saved email. You can also attach the saved original to check@signet7.io and the same result comes back to you by email. That address only answers checks; it is not a mailbox anyone reads.

What you do

Four steps.

1. Save the email

Save the original out of your mail app. If it came with a Signet7-sealed.eml attachment, keep that attachment instead. Do not forward it — forwarding can strip what the check needs, and most phones cannot export it, so use a computer. See where the signature lives.

2. Upload it

Open the Live check in any browser. No account. Upload the file you just saved. If you have the Signet7 pane in Outlook, you can check the open message instead. You would not do this for junk mail — you do it for the email asking you to move money or change an account.

3. Read the wording answer

Words match, words do not match, or no seal. No seal is ordinary mail — it simply means there is no signature to compare against. Checking does not create a seal.

4. Read the sender answer

Listed for this address, not listed, or the listing does not match.

When it is worth doing

The emails that are worth 60 seconds

The pattern is the same everywhere: someone asks you to send money somewhere new, or to change where money already goes.

Accounts payable

A supplier writes in with new bank details for an invoice. Check the wording before you update the vendor record.

Legal and settlements

The other side changes settlement or disbursement instructions late in the process.

Property and title

A payoff or closing statement arrives by email, with figures that must not have moved.

Payroll

An employee asks to redirect a direct deposit. A listing covering payroll does not cover accounts payable — each address is separate.

Afterwards

What you keep

The answer is not just something you read and forget. Signet7 gives you a signed file recording what was checked, what the wording answer was, whether the address was listed, and when you asked. That file is the record. Signet7 does not keep a copy of your email for you.
