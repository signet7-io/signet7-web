What the recipient does

Four steps. One result.

1. Keep the email

Save the original. A forward can drop what the check needs. A phone often cannot export it. Use a desk if you have to.

2. Open the check

Open the live check. No account. Verified is not safe. Unknown is not fraud.

3. Check VSN

Look up whether the signing key is still bound to that organization at qual.signet7.io/vsn. Today only Signet7's own domain is in that lookup.

4. Read the result

Matched, unmatched, or unknown. Then you decide. Verified does not mean safe. Unknown does not mean fraud.
