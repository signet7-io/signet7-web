Evidence standard

Match each statement to the right proof.

A capability may be described as current when

  • The exact behavior exists in the pinned candidate.
  • Tests cover its important positive and negative paths.
  • Its trust, scope, and failure boundaries are documented.
  • Any deployment claim has environment-specific operating evidence.
  • The claim register permits the wording for the intended audience.

These are not substitutes for proof

  • A product vision, architecture diagram, or future-state workflow.
  • A configuration option without an accepted operating record.
  • A demo that omits unsupported MIME, attachment, or status cases.
  • A hash, signature, or anchor without independent trust inputs.
  • A planned package, price, SLA, certification, or customer conversation.
Product center

Keep the policy gate and caller enforcement separate.

The repository includes action binding, policy evaluation, HTTP/MCP tooling, and an agent harness. These are now the product center, but the caller remains responsible for enforcing the result and performing the downstream action.
