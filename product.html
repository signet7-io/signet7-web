The check — every human

No account. No install. You do not check junk. You check the email that asks you to move money or change an account. One person and a thousand people use the same door.

Inbox Watch — one company inbox

A program on one PC that sits on the company inbox that pays bills. Ordinary mail stays quiet. It shouts if a seal is torn. It does not send. It is not for every laptop.

VSN — your company can be looked up

Verifiable Sender Network (VSN) lists the key that seals your mail. Enroll when you seal outbound. Today the public lookup lists Signet7’s own domain. Customer companies are not in it until you enroll.
