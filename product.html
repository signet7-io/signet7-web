Implemented in the local candidate

The email and commercial control planes are real—and bounded.

Current source capability

  • Versioned s7-email-1 profile for supported RFC 5322/MIME input.
  • Canonical protected headers; protected text and HTML alternatives.
  • Attachment bytes and declared metadata represented through explicit manifests.
  • Signing, verification, sender/key binding outcomes, historical and current status checks, expiry and replay semantics.
  • HTTP and browser recipient verifier with a claim-safe proof card and downloadable receipt.
  • Five-program catalog, Stripe-test control plane, account/pricing UI, and server-side entitlement gates.

Current limits

  • No production service, customer deployment, SLA, independent penetration test, or cryptographic review.
  • Unsupported MIME structures and provider transformations can return unsupported or invalid results.
  • The browser compose page is a synthetic pilot surface: it does not establish durable managed sender key custody.
  • A verifier-response receipt is not automatically an external anchor, legal conclusion, or complete-history proof.
Sender wedge

Start where consequential email repeats.

The first direct design-partner wedge is freelancers and microbusinesses protecting invoices and payment instructions. The system protects declared components; it does not validate bank ownership, approve payments, or determine whether a request is prudent.

Invoices and payment instructions

Protect subject, sender, supported body alternatives, and attachments; give clients a free check and a receipt.

Contracts and scope changes

Make later changes to covered clauses or attached documents detectable while preserving exact coverage limits.

Institutional B2B2C

Let title, legal, financial, medical, employment, and insurance partners sponsor verification for the people relying on their messages.

What Signet7 does not mean

Verification is evidence, not a verdict on reality.

Not a generic secure inbox

No spam filtering, malware scanning, mailbox takeover prevention, VPN, password manager, or confidentiality claim.

Not an intent detector

A valid signature does not prove the human typist, advice, transaction, or request is truthful, authorized, lawful, or wise.

Not automatic fraud classification

Unverified does not mean fraudulent. Verified does not mean safe to act. Use independent callback and transaction controls where consequences demand them.
