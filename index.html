Trust the email before you act.

Signet7 is the trust layer for consequential email—used by organizations, professionals, and the individuals who rely on their messages. Before you act on an important email, Signet7 is designed to help you check who sealed it, identify which components were protected, detect changes to those components, and preserve a portable verification receipt.

The moment Signet7 owns

Appearance is not proof.

Display names, familiar branding, writing style, apparent addresses, and old conversation threads can all look convincing. When an email could cost money, change rights, expose sensitive information, or become evidence later, recipients need a calm way to inspect stronger proof before acting.

01

Invoices and payment instructions

Freelancers, bookkeepers, contractors, and microbusinesses can protect the content clients are expected to rely on. Signet7 does not approve payment or prove bank details are prudent.

02

Contracts and consequential changes

Protect covered clauses, scope changes, notices, and attachments so a recipient can detect later changes and retain a result.

03

Institutional instructions

Title, legal, financial, medical, employment, and insurance workflows can sponsor free recipient checks through bounded B2B2C pilots.

Free recipient verification

Verification should not require a purchase decision.

The approved direction keeps recipient verification free wherever operationally viable. Trusted senders, professionals, businesses, and enterprises fund signing, managed identity, lifecycle, administration, integrations, support, and retention.

CheckUpload or forward a supported protected message to a compatible verifier.
UnderstandSee integrity, sender/key binding, status, coverage, and warnings separately.
KeepDownload a versioned receipt of the verifier response where supported.
DecideUse ordinary safety checks as well; verified content can still be mistaken or malicious.
Current candidate truth

A working email evidence profile. Not a live managed service.

Implemented in the local product candidate

  • Versioned s7-email-1 signing and verification for supported MIME messages.
  • Protected text and HTML alternatives plus attachment bytes and declared metadata.
  • Explicit coverage manifests, sender/key binding outcomes, lifecycle checks, and optional evidence recording.
  • Five-program catalog, Stripe-test entitlement control plane, account/pricing UI, and paid-operation gates.
  • Local Gmail, Outlook, and browser-extension packages with free verification and program-aware sender paths.

Not established as complete

  • Production provider compatibility, recipient inbox indicators, mobile distribution, or a customer deployment.
  • Durable managed sender custody through the current browser compose demonstration.
  • The complete managed VSN enrollment, directory, recovery, revocation, administration, support, and operating service.
  • Universal identity, truthful-content, malware-safety, fraud-prevention, confidentiality, or legal-admissibility guarantees.
Interpretation rule: “unverified” means the required evidence was not established. It does not mean “fraudulent.” “Verified” applies only to the stated cryptographic and trust facts; it does not mean “safe to act.”
Verifiable Sender Network

Keep the enterprise-grade trust infrastructure.

VSN remains Signet7’s managed trust layer for enrollment, discovery, key status, rotation, revocation, recovery, administration, scale, support, and abuse controls. The pivot broadens who benefits; it does not demote the infrastructure required to make trust dependable.

Roadmap, not current-service claim

The source contains trust-binding, enrollment, local lifecycle, and DNS-discovery foundations. The complete managed VSN service and its operating controls remain planned.
