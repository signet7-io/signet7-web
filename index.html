Why you are here

An email can look ordinary and still be the last step before money moves. Signet7 is the check you run first — so you know what can be proven before you act.

What it is

The trust layer for consequential email. Not a new inbox. Not a spam filter. A last look at the sender and the sealed parts of a message before you send money or follow an instruction.

What it offers

A recipient check. Clear states: bound, self-signed, or unresolved. Evidence you can keep. The Verifiable Sender Network (VSN) binds a signing key to a domain or organization and records whether that binding is still active.

What it does not do

It does not move money, replace a callback, or decide for you. Verified does not mean safe. Unknown does not mean fraud. You already have a product: call the person.

The missing control point

From an ordinary email to a decision you may regret.

Signet7 lets the recipient inspect supported sender and content evidence before they act. Agents can later consume the same evidence.

01

Instruction enters

Evaluate supported signature evidence, declared content coverage, and the configured sender/key relationship.

02

Policy decides

Bind and normalize the exact proposed action. Known high-risk action families require stronger evidence; unknown actions fail closed.

03

Caller enforces

Return allow or block and seal the decision record. The integrating agent, workflow, or tool runner enforces the result.

Current boundary: Signet7 returns and records the policy decision. It does not independently intercept every banking, purchasing, or command system.

Signature-bound sender identity

A look-alike sender should not inherit an authorized key.

Signet7 checks whether supported content was signed by the exact key bound to the claimed sender in the configured trust source. A spoofed look-alike without that private key cannot satisfy the same bound-signature check.

This is a cryptographic anti-spoofing control, not broad phishing detection. Compromised keys, bad enrollment, bad policy, and compromised callers remain material risks.

Portable decision evidence

Built for the review that comes later.

Signed, hash-chained records, detached export, offline verification, evidence bundles, retention-policy events, and optional external anchoring support audit, investigation, and regulated workflows.

What was evaluated

Canonical action, trust state, policy result, protected identifiers, and record chronology.

What can travel

Signed exports and bundles with manifests, checksums, signer inputs, and expected-head evidence.

What remains contextual

Customer policy, custody, completeness, deployment, retention, and legal applicability still control the compliance conclusion.

Evidence for compliance-grade workflows is not a certification of HIPAA, SOX, SEC, EU AI Act, or any other regime.

API/MCP-first

No replacement mailbox. No traditional security gateway. No endpoint agent.

Connect the current decision interface to the agent or workflow that proposes the action. Integration, caller enforcement, and an operated Signet7 service are still required.

Verifiable Sender Network (VSN)

Bind a signing key to a domain. Check whether that binding still holds.

VSN is Signet7's sender-trust layer: it records which organization or domain a signing key belongs to, and whether that binding is still active. A lookup and admin pages are live at qual.signet7.io/vsn on an isolated qualification tenant. The only domain in that lookup today is Signet7's own (signet7.io), added by dual-control. Customer domains are not in it, and records are not publicly listed.

Verified does not mean safe. Customer enrollment, rotation, revocation, recovery, and support are still not an operating customer network.
