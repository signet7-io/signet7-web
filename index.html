What it is

Signet7 is the cryptographic check for high-stakes email, powered by our Verifiable Sender Network (VSN).

It verifies whether a message is bound to the claimed sender, and whether the protected words — including payment or account details — still match the seal.

You get a clear result and a signed file you can produce later. The same evidence can gate actions proposed by people or AI agents before irreversible steps.

Who this is for

Businesses that move money or change accounts because an email said so.

Whoever got the email can open the Signet7 live check with no account and no install. We built it for the office that then has to pay, change an account, or follow the email instruction.

Settlement, retainer, or “updated wiring.” Inspect the seal. Keep the record.

A record you can produce

You hand them the check. Not a verdict.

After you check a message, you save the result. When an auditor, insurer, partner, or court asks what you received, you produce that file.

1. Save the result

Keep a file you can open later, even offline. It is signed, so you can tell if that file was altered.

2. What the file holds

Who the message was tied to. Whether the protected words still matched. The date and time of that check.

3. The original stays put

The email stays in their mailbox. Signet7 is the check, not a second inbox.

Together, these show the message we received, what the seal said at the time, and that the record has not been edited.

You produce it. They weigh it.
