What it is

Signet7 is the cryptographic check for high-stakes email — powered by VSN.

It verifies whether a message is bound to the claimed sender through the Verifiable Sender Network (VSN), and whether the protected content — including payment, account, or other critical details — still matches the seal. You get a clear result and a tamper-evident record you control and can produce later.

The same evidence can gate actions proposed by people or AI agents before irreversible steps are taken.

Built for law firms, title companies, finance teams, and anyone who needs to verify consequential email before acting — and keep the proof.
