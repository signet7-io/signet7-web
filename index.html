What it is

Signet7 is the cryptographic check for high-stakes email, powered by our Verifiable Sender Network (VSN).

It verifies whether a message is bound to the claimed sender, and whether the protected words — including payment or account details — still match the seal.

You get a clear result and a signed file you can produce later. The same evidence can gate actions proposed by people or AI agents before irreversible steps.
