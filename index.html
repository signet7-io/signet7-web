Cryptographic agent-action gating

Gate what the agent does.
Not just what lands in the inbox.

When an AI agent can propose a wire, purchase, refund, transfer, or system command, inbox filtering ends too early. Signet7 verifies supported instruction evidence, checks the exact proposed action against policy, and seals the decision before the integrating system enforces it.

Inbox security stops at delivery.

Agent risk starts when a message becomes a transaction, tool call, approval, or command.

The missing control point

From ordinary instruction to consequential action.

Email can look routine while carrying the instruction that moves money or changes a system. Signet7 puts an inspectable policy decision between what the agent receives and what the agent proposes to do.

01

Instruction enters

Evaluate supported signature evidence, declared content coverage, and the configured sender/key relationship.

02

Policy decides

Bind and normalize the exact proposed action. Known high-risk action families require stronger evidence; unknown actions fail closed.

03

Caller enforces

Return allow or block and seal the decision record. The integrating agent, workflow, or tool runner enforces the result.

Signature-bound sender identity

A look-alike sender should not inherit an authorized key.

Signet7 checks whether supported content was signed by the exact key bound to the claimed sender in the configured trust source. A spoofed look-alike without that private key cannot satisfy the same bound-signature check.

This is a cryptographic anti-spoofing control, not broad phishing detection. Compromised keys, bad enrollment, bad policy, and compromised callers remain material risks.

Portable decision evidence

Built for the review that comes later.

Signed, hash-chained records, detached export, offline verification, evidence bundles, retention-policy events, and optional external anchoring support audit, investigation, and regulated workflows.

What was evaluated

Canonical action, trust state, policy result, protected identifiers, and record chronology.

What can travel

Signed exports and bundles with manifests, checksums, signer inputs, and expected-head evidence.

What remains contextual

Customer policy, custody, completeness, deployment, retention, and legal applicability still control the compliance conclusion.

Evidence for compliance-grade workflows is not a certification of HIPAA, SOX, SEC, EU AI Act, or any other regime.

API/MCP-first

No replacement mailbox. No traditional security gateway. No endpoint agent.

Connect the current decision interface to the agent or workflow that proposes the action. Integration, caller enforcement, and an operated Signet7 service are still required.
