What nobody else does

Nobody else puts the whole check together.

Sender

See who it is bound to.

The company or person the message claims to be from — not the costume of the address.

Sealed words

See whether the words still match.

Including payment details. Hashes, not a guess. Then a plain answer: bound, not yet approved, or unresolved.

The record

Keep the proof. Take it with you.

Signed and chained so a later change is visible. Export a bundle you can verify later, even offline. The 7 is the seven-year record standard — not a promise every email is stored for seven years.

Finance. Title. Law. Construction. Payroll. Anyone who would wire because a message asked.
