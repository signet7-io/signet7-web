The missing control point

From an ordinary email to a decision you may regret.

Signet7 lets the recipient inspect supported sender and content evidence before they act. Agents can later consume the same evidence.

Current boundary: Signet7 returns and records the policy decision. It does not independently intercept every banking, purchasing, or command system.

Signature-bound sender identity

A look-alike sender should not inherit an authorized key.

Signet7 checks whether supported content was signed by the exact key bound to the claimed sender in the configured trust source. A spoofed look-alike without that private key cannot satisfy the same bound-signature check.

This is a cryptographic anti-spoofing control, not broad phishing detection. Compromised keys, bad enrollment, bad policy, and compromised callers remain material risks.

Verifiable Sender Network (VSN)

Bind a signing key to a domain. Check whether that binding still holds.

VSN is Signet7's sender-trust layer: it records which organization or domain a signing key belongs to, and whether that binding is still active. A lookup and admin pages are live at qual.signet7.io/vsn on an isolated qualification tenant. The only domain in that lookup today is Signet7's own (signet7.io), added by dual-control. Customer domains are not in it, and records are not publicly listed.

Verified does not mean safe. Customer enrollment, rotation, revocation, recovery, and support are still not an operating customer network.
