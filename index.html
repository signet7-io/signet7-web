Trust layer for consequential email

Here's what he does, and how it affects you.

He is the last look before you act on an important email. Not a new inbox. Not a spam filter. A check you can keep.

Spam filters, encryption, and registered mail each do one slice. Nobody else puts the whole check together.

You already have a product: call the person. Signet7 is the last look before money or a decision moves.

Verified does not mean safe. Unknown does not mean fraud.

What he does

Sender
See whether the message is bound to the company or person it claims to be from.
Sealed words
See whether the protected parts still match the seal — including payment details — using hashes, not a guess.
Plain answer
Bound, not yet approved, or unresolved. Then you decide.
Risky actions
Known high-risk actions can require a bound sender. Unknown actions fail closed. The caller still enforces.
The record
What was checked is signed and chained so a later change is visible.
Take it with you
Export a bundle you can verify later, even offline.
The 7
The seven-year record standard. Keep evidence you can still produce later — not a promise every email is stored for seven years.
Optional VSN
Look up whether a company's signing key is still active. The check today is hosted on qual.
