Do you need a download?

Usually not. Here is every route.

The people receiving your email are not required to install anything — a thousand of them are covered because they can open the check in a browser. Signet7 desktop only ever covers the work addresses you name, on office computers.

Browser check — nothing to install

The route for everyone on the other side. verify.signet7.io/email/verify, no account. Upload the Signet7-sealed.eml attachment if there is one, otherwise the saved email. Or attach the saved original to check@signet7.io and the answer comes back by email.

Signet7 pane for Outlook — optional

Files are hosted at signet7.io/outlook. Save the manifest, open aka.ms/olksideload, and choose Add from File. It is not an AppSource listing, and the newer Apps area cannot upload XML. The Sign and send email button attaches Signet7-sealed.eml.

Signet7 desktop — this page

One Windows, Mac, or Linux computer per set of named addresses. Unsigned preview. Both jobs live in this app: checking what arrives, and optionally signing what you send through 127.0.0.1 port 2525. That hop seals the email itself.

Company account — no download

Sign in by email link at account.signet7.io/account. Registering is free.

Lab builds

A browser extension, a Gmail add-on, and an Android build exist as unpacked sideloads in the client tree. None of them is the product, and none is in a store.

Not in any app store

Signet7 is not published in any consumer or enterprise app marketplace. If you find something claiming to be Signet7 in one, it is not us. The zip on this page and the pane files at signet7.io/outlook are the only distributions.

For IT

Rolling this out

  1. Push the Signet7 pane for Outlook from Microsoft 365 admin → Settings → Integrated apps, assigning it to a group as Available, Optional, or Fixed. The pane files live at signet7.io/outlook. Steps: IT push to laptops.
  2. Do not push Signet7 desktop to every laptop. The zip is unsigned, there is no Intune MSI, and both Windows and Mac will warn. Install it only on the computers handling named addresses.
  3. Choose which work addresses can move money, jobs, or clients. Not every inbox. Register the app in Microsoft Entra; Graph consent still requires a person.
  4. Ordinary mail stays quiet, and quiet means it is working. On a named address, signing happens automatically over SMTP at 127.0.0.1 port 2525. See where the signature lives.
  5. Tell everyone else the one thing they need: if an email asks you to move money, upload it at verify.signet7.io/email/verify before acting.

It is the same on Windows, Mac, and Linux. It is not Exchange, not a mail app, and not a spam filter — Outlook stays Outlook and Apple Mail stays Apple Mail. Worked examples: what a clerk actually does.
