Draft privacy notice DRAFT — NON-OPERATIVE

A privacy notice that matches what Signet7 actually processes.

This is the customer-facing privacy notice for the current Signet7 candidate. It is not an executed DPA, not a formed-entity commitment, and not a claim that a production service is operating.

1. Who we are

Signet7 is a pre-formation project. The controller or business will be the formed entity named in an executed order. Until then this notice describes the current candidate so data collection is not hidden.

2. Data we collect

  • This static website: HTML, CSS, and images only. No signup, payment, or message-upload form exists in this source.
  • Hosting metadata: a host such as GitHub Pages may log IP address, user agent, path, time, and security events. That is host behavior, not a Signet7 form.
  • Recipient verify: the service may receive supported raw message bytes in memory to hash and check. Default public verify does not keep the raw email.
  • Optional evidence: if an authorized operator turns evidence on, Signet7 stores protected identifiers and digests, not the raw body, submitted signature, or submitted key.
  • Mailbox agent: a customer-installed agent may read mailbox metadata through IMAP or Microsoft Graph on the customer's machine. It is designed not to keep mail bodies.
  • Account and test billing: tenant, program, subscription identifiers, and event IDs. No card numbers. Live billing is off.

3. Why we process it

To verify supported sender and content evidence, operate an optional action gate, secure the service, prevent abuse, and, if separately authorized, project a test subscription. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

4. Artificial intelligence

Signet7 is not a chatbot and does not generate email or marketing copy. An optional policy engine can record whether a named action was allowed or blocked. See AI use.

5. Data providers

Current and candidate providers are listed on Data providers. Stripe is not an active subprocessor until a live offer is authorized.

6. Retention and deletion

Uploads and raw verify payloads are not retained by default. Transient files are deleted after processing. Send a deletion request through the published contact channel or POST /api/v1/privacy/deletion-request on an operated instance. Legal holds, if any, are recorded separately and are not a blanket keep-forever rule. See Cancel.

7. Rights

Depending on applicable law, a person may request access, correction, deletion, export, or restriction. Identity checks apply. This draft does not decide GDPR, state, or sector roles until the entity and deployment are fixed.

8. Children

The product is not directed to children. Do not submit a child's personal information.

9. Security and public storage

Declared object stores are configured with public-access blocks. Source defaults are not a live-account audit. Do not treat this notice as a certification.

10. Contact

Use the published contact page or discussion inbox. Those channels are not formal legal notice until a formed provider names a privacy address. Do not send live payment messages or secrets.
