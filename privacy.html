Current candidate record

Protected identifiers, not a promise of content-free operation.

The current decision path is designed to persist protected identifiers, fingerprints, action/provenance/policy fields, metadata, and evidence-chain information rather than storing the submitted body, signature, or key verbatim in that decision record.

Important boundary

The service necessarily receives inputs to evaluate them, and other routes, logs, deployment components, exports, test fixtures, integrations, or future email workflows may handle different data. The exact deployed flow must be reviewed before making a privacy claim.

Product status: the current decision route receives instruction text, exact proposed action, sender and message identifiers, tenant/agent context, and optional signature/key material. The current email routes can also receive supported raw message bytes. The deployed operator must still prove request logging, temporary-file, backup, access, deletion, regional, and retention behavior before making a privacy commitment.
Commercial candidate status: the local product branch includes Stripe-test checkout/portal adapters and stores a minimized tenant subscription projection plus event identifiers. It does not store raw webhook payloads or payment methods. No live Stripe deployment, public customer identity/recovery system, final privacy notice, or customer processing authorization is established.
Verifiable Sender Network (VSN)

Managed trust creates a lifecycle record.

A complete VSN service is expected to process organization, sender, administrator, key, trust-status, rotation, revocation, recovery, verification, and abuse-prevention data. The managed service is not complete today, so its final data flows and commitments remain subject to design, security, privacy, legal, and operational approval.

Enroll

Define what evidence is required, who may act for an organization, and how errors or disputes are corrected.

Maintain

Record status changes, rotation, revocation, recovery, administrative actions, and applicable audit history.

Resolve

Expose only the information a verifier needs, with rate limits, abuse controls, status semantics, and privacy review.
