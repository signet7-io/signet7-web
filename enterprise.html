Setup

Four moves. No theater.

1. Staff use the live check

Every human. No install. They do not check junk. They check the email that asks them to move money or change an account. Same door as one person.

2. One Watch on AP

One program on one PC, on the inbox that pays bills. Ordinary mail stays quiet. Not a thousand Watches.

3. IT can push Outlook

Then the check happens on the open message. People do not leave mail. Sideload works today. Org-wide push is a Microsoft 365 admin step. Not AppSource.

4. Enroll VSN when you seal outbound

So outsiders can look up whether that key is still yours. Today the public lookup lists Signet7’s own domain. Customer companies are not in it until you enroll. Checkout is not live.
