FAQ

The short answers.

I got an important email. What do I do?

Keep the original message. Open the live check. Read what Signet7 can prove. You do not check junk. You check the email that asks you to move money or change an account.

If the words match, is it safe?

No. Signet7 reports what it can prove about the sender and the words. It does not approve payment, scan for viruses, or name the person at the keyboard.

What does no seal mean?

Ordinary mail. Checking does not stamp a new seal. Unknown or unsealed is not an attack by itself.

Do I write email inside Signet7?

No. Write in Outlook, Apple Mail, or Thunderbird. Same on Windows, Mac, and Linux. If you opt in, Signet7 can stamp the message you already wrote. See the living scenarios.

What does Watch do?

Watch sits on the named company inboxes you connect — typically AP, payroll, legal. Not Watch on every staff laptop. Ordinary mail stays quiet. It shouts if a seal is torn, a named vendor arrives unsealed, or a listing doesn’t match this address. It does not send. Recipients never install it. Anyone still uses the live check on any device.

Why do I have to go to a website for every check?

The website is the no-install door. If that is all you use, each important email is a trip to that page. After a company account, pin the Outlook pane so changing mail re-checks. Inbox Watch does that trip for named company inboxes. Recipients still never install.

What are the three jobs?

The site checks a message. Inbox Watch watches incoming on the named company inboxes you connect. Seal stamps outgoing. Account is not the Watch app. Sideload is not Watch. Recipients never install Watch.

Ready, Preview, Not yet

Check: Ready. Inbox Watch: Preview (unsigned). Seal: Not yet as a signed product. Pay: visible and off. People and agents use the same check before money moves.

Account, Watch app, watched inbox

A company account is a Signet7 login. The Watch app is software on a PC. A watched inbox is a named mailbox that app or hosted Watch sits on. Large companies connect several named inboxes. That is not Watch on every laptop.

Where are the seven-year records?

There is no seven-year default. A public check does not keep the letter. You can download a receipt for your files. If Signet7 records a ledger row, that is content-minimized evidence (hashes, keys, status), not the readable email, in operator storage with human-approved WORM retention. US tax and audit rules sometimes talk about years of records — they do not make Signet7 a 7-year archive by itself.

What does the other company get?

If you are using Signet7, they do not buy Watch. They get the free live check on any device, and a Signet7 invite so they can look you up — only between you and them — until they register themselves. That is the two-way link. A company can have more than one listing. Each listing is a key plus the addresses assigned to it.

Should Signet7 store sealed files?

No, not by default. Keep the signed file. That file is the record. A public check does not keep the letter. Optional long-term storage of sealed mail is not a product today.
