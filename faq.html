FAQ

What a person actually needs to know.

Why would I bother?

You already have a product: call the person. Spam filters feel like email security. Payment-detail fraud is rare until it is not. Signet7 is a seatbelt for a crash you have not had. Use it before you change where money goes. Verified is not safe. Unknown is not fraud.

I got an important email. What do I do?

Keep the original message. Open the live check. Acknowledge Terms. Drop in the .eml or paste the raw source. Read what Signet7 can prove. Then still use a known callback before you pay or send secrets.

Do I install an app?

Recipients do not install. Use a browser. iPhone Mail often cannot export .eml; use a desktop if you cannot get the original file.

If it says verified, is it safe?

No. Verified is not safe. Unknown is not fraud. Signet7 reports sender/key and protected-content facts. It does not approve payment, scan malware, or name the human at the keyboard.

Do I write email inside Signet7?

No. Write in Outlook, Apple Mail, or Thunderbird. Same on Windows, Mac, and Linux. If you opt in, Signet7 can stamp the message you already wrote. See the living scenarios.

Can I buy this today?

No public checkout. No prices on this site. Design-partner interest goes to justin.daines@signet7.io.

Is there a live sender network?

The Verifiable Sender Network (VSN) binds a signing key to a domain or organization and records whether that binding is still active. A lookup is live on our qualification site. The only domain in it today is Signet7's own. Customer companies are not in that lookup.
