What a person actually needs to know.

Source: FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center, 2025 Annual Report. Reported losses, not Signet7 results. Read the IC3 report.

Why would I bother?

Call them on a number you already have — not the one in the email. Spam filters feel like email security. Payment-detail fraud is rare until it is not. Signet7 is a seatbelt for a crash you have not had. Use it before you change where money goes. Verified is not safe. Unknown is not fraud.

I got an important email. What do I do?

Keep the original message. Open the live check. Read what Signet7 can prove. Then still call a number you already have before you pay or send secrets.

Do I install an app?

Recipients do not install. Use a browser. iPhone Mail often cannot export .eml; use a desktop if you cannot get the original file.

If it says verified, is it safe?

No. Verified is not safe. Unknown is not fraud. Signet7 reports what it can prove about the sender and the words. It does not approve payment, scan for viruses, or name the person at the keyboard.

Do I write email inside Signet7?

No. Write in Outlook, Apple Mail, or Thunderbird. Same on Windows, Mac, and Linux. If you opt in, Signet7 can stamp the message you already wrote. See the living scenarios.
