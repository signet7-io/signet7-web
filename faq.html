FAQ

The short answers.

I got an important email. What do I do?

Keep the original message. Open the live check. Read what Signet7 can prove. Then still call a number you already have before you pay or send secrets. You do not check junk. You check the email that asks you to move money or change an account.

If it says verified, is it safe?

No. Verified is not safe. Unknown is not fraud. Signet7 reports what it can prove about the sender and the words. It does not approve payment, scan for viruses, or name the person at the keyboard.

Do I write email inside Signet7?

No. Write in Outlook, Apple Mail, or Thunderbird. Same on Windows, Mac, and Linux. If you opt in, Signet7 can stamp the message you already wrote. See the living scenarios.

What does Watch do?

Watch is a program on one PC that sits on one company inbox — usually the inbox that pays bills. Ordinary mail stays quiet. It shouts if a seal is torn, or if a named vendor arrives unsealed. It does not send. Recipients never install it. Staff of a thousand still use the live check unless IT pushes Outlook.

Why do I have to go to a website for every check?

The website is the no-install door. If that is all you use, each important email is a trip to that page. After a company account: pin the Outlook pane so changing mail re-checks; Watch does that trip for the one inbox. Invite the people you already email so they can be looked up — that mail comes from Signet7, not inside your business letter. That is the difference between going to the factory and using the car.

What is VSN?

Verifiable Sender Network (VSN) is how a company lists the key that seals its mail so outsiders can look it up. Enroll when you seal outbound. Today the public lookup lists Signet7’s own domain. Customer companies are not in it until you enroll.
