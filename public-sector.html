How it works

Three steps, and no change to your mail system

1. Save the email

The approver saves the original email out of Outlook, Apple Mail, or Thunderbird. Nothing is installed to do this.

2. Upload it to the Live check

They open the Live check in a browser and upload that file. No account and no login.

3. Read the answer, then act

Signet7 says whether the wording still matches what was signed, and whether the sender's address is on record. The approver keeps the signed result as proof.

Signet7 does not sit in your mail flow. It is not a mail gateway, it does not replace Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, and it does not filter, hold, or rewrite messages. Nothing breaks if Signet7 is unavailable — the email still arrives exactly as it does today. See the walkthrough.

Being straight with you

What Signet7 does not do

It does not say an email is safe

A clean result means the wording is unchanged and the sender's address is on record. It is not approval to pay. Your call-back and approval rules still apply.

It does not replace your filter

Spam and malware filtering, multi-factor sign-in, and separation of duties all stay exactly where they are. Signet7 is the last look before an irreversible step.

It does not cover every channel

Telephone calls, text messages, portal messages, and printed paper are outside what Signet7 can check. It works on email.
