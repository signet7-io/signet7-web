Trust & evidence

Cryptographic facts, without magical conclusions.

Signet7 separates signature validity, sender/key binding, protected-content integrity, policy outcome, caller enforcement, evidence custody, and legal interpretation. Each answers a different question.

Signature-bound sender identity

A key relationship that can be checked.

Bound

The exact supplied key matches a configured tenant sender binding or accepted domain trust source. This establishes only that configured cryptographic relationship.

Self-signed

The signature may be cryptographically valid under the supplied key, but the key is not approved for the claimed sender. It does not earn a verified sender relationship.

Unresolved

The required signature or binding evidence was not established. This is absence of supported proof, not proof of fraud.

Anti-spoofing boundary: a look-alike sender without the authorized private key cannot satisfy the same bound-signature check. This does not block every phishing technique or account compromise.

What a valid result means

Possession, coverage, binding, and policy are separate.

SignatureThe corresponding private key signed the exact canonical input.
BindingThe exact public key matched the configured sender relationship or accepted trust source.
CoverageOnly the components declared by the supported profile were checked.
PolicyThe exact canonical action met the current narrow rule for that trust state.
EnforcementThe caller, not Signet7, performed or refused the downstream action.

Compliance boundary

Evidence support is not certification.

SEC recordkeeping, SOX-related audit retention, HIPAA documentation retention, and EU AI Act logging/retention duties can create demand for durable, defensible records. They do not mandate Signet7 or regulate every message, action, actor, or workflow.

Signet7 can contribute

Portable cryptographic evidence about the inputs, action, policy result, and chronology the gate recorded.

The customer still controls

Policy design, access, identity proofing, deployment, custody, record classification, retention, and enforcement.

The law still controls

Applicability, required record content, admissibility, regulated role, jurisdiction, and qualified interpretation.

Not approved: “HIPAA email,” “SOX compliant,” “SEC approved,” “EU AI Act compliant,” “court-grade,” or “seven years by default.”

Never collapse these meanings

Verified is not safe. Unresolved is not fraudulent.

Supported conclusions

  • Signature validity under the exact supplied key
  • Configured sender/key binding result
  • Declared protected-component integrity result
  • Exact-action policy decision
  • Recorded evidence chronology within the stated inputs

Unsupported conclusions

  • Which human physically acted
  • Business authority under every rule
  • Truthfulness, prudence, lawfulness, or safety
  • Malware or ransomware absence
  • Universal compliance or legal admissibility
