Consequential email should be checked.

Signet7 is being developed as the trust layer for consequential email: check supported sender and content evidence, then keep the result. AI agents are a later consumer of that evidence. A qualification VSN check is hosted. It is not a customer-enrolled managed network.

The mission

Give important email a check before action.

AI agents can now turn ordinary-looking instructions into wires, purchases, refunds, transfers, or commands. Signet7 is intended to complement inbox, identity, approval, and downstream authorization controls with signature-bound instruction evidence, exact-action policy, and portable decision records.

PlatformSignet7 is the trust layer for consequential email.
InstructionConsequential email is the primary signed-instruction carrier and human checkpoint.
ActionThe exact proposed action is bound, evaluated, and recorded before caller enforcement.
VSNA qualification VSN check is hosted. The complete managed network is not a customer-enrolled service.

How the team works

Product direction and product evidence stay separate.

01

Define precisely

State the product goal, coverage profile, trust assumptions, user result, and managed trust infrastructure without letting future-state language become a current capability claim.

02

Build and test

Implement versioned contracts, negative cases, lifecycle behavior, provider transformations, evidence handling, and operational controls.

03

Release with proof

Promote only after technical, security, privacy, legal, operational, and claim owners accept the exact statement and supporting evidence.

Current stage

A release-candidate decision gate, not a finished managed service.

The repository demonstrates action-bound signatures, sender/key trust resolution, narrow fail-closed policy, HTTP/MCP decisions, signed decision chronology, portable evidence, and the s7-email-1 protected-message profile. Direction-specific policy, universal tool interception, production operations, durable managed sender custody, and the complete VSN service remain to be built and qualified.
