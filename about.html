Important email should be checkable.

Signet7 is being developed as the trust layer for consequential email—used by organizations, professionals, and the individuals who rely on their messages—to check sender/key evidence, detect changes to protected components, and preserve a result for later review.

The mission

Give high-trust email a verifiable evidence layer.

Email remains central to approvals, instructions, transactions, notices, and records. Signet7 is intended to complement existing transport, gateway, reputation, encryption, and user controls with explicit sender/key trust, declared protected-content coverage, and portable evidence.

PlatformSignet7 is the umbrella email trust offering.
CoreSigning, verification, and evidence may be operated in a portable verification model.
VSNThe Verifiable Sender Network is the managed trust infrastructure for enterprise scale.
ExtensionAI-agent, enterprise-system, ERP/CRM, compliance-pipeline, and broader digital-trust uses remain supporting or future applications.

How the team works

Product direction and product evidence stay separate.

01

Define precisely

State the product goal, coverage profile, trust assumptions, user result, and managed trust infrastructure without letting future-state language become a current capability claim.

02

Build and test

Implement versioned contracts, negative cases, lifecycle behavior, provider transformations, evidence handling, and operational controls.

03

Release with proof

Promote only after technical, security, privacy, legal, operational, and claim owners accept the exact statement and supporting evidence.

Current stage

A release-candidate foundation, not a finished email service.

The repository now demonstrates the s7-email-1 protected-message profile, supported MIME and attachment coverage, sender/key trust paths, lifecycle/status outcomes, tenant-scoped evidence, export, and browser/API recipient verification. Production provider interoperability, durable managed sender custody, mobile distribution, and the complete managed VSN service remain to be built and qualified.
