The name

A signet is a seal. The 7 is the long memory.

You put a seal on a letter so it can still be proven years later, when an auditor, partner, or court asks what you received.

You need to know it is authentic.

Was it bound to who they claim? Do the protected words still match?

You need proof of exactly what you received.
