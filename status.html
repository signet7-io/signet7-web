What this means for you

Use it for the check. Plan the rest with us.

Safe to use now

Checking an email you received is finished work. It costs nothing, needs no account, and asks nothing of the person who sent you the email.

Worth piloting now

Signing your own outgoing email and running the desktop app on a few named addresses. Expect extra OS warnings until signing is finished and expect to talk to us.

Do not plan around yet

A signed contract, a published price, or a certification you can hand an auditor. Those are not ready, and we would rather you heard it here.
