One trust product. Five program levels.

Recipient verification stays free where operationally viable. Paid programs add sender sealing, evidence, lifecycle, administration, integrations, automation, retention, and enterprise dispatch. The paywall never turns ordinary unsealed or unknown email into a fraud verdict.
