Commercial direction

Price the sender habit. Keep recipient checks free.

Commodity email security is priced per user or mailbox. Signet7's value sits at the consequential control point: governed agents, policy decisions, managed identity, decision evidence, integrations, and operational support.

Governed agents

Why not $4.99 per inbox?

Email trust spending validates the need. It does not define this category.

Businesses already pay monthly for domain, mailbox, and email-security controls. Signet7 should not enter that commodity comparison. The differentiated value is cryptographic agent governance: checking the signed instruction, binding the exact proposed action, recording policy, and preserving evidence.

Founding design partner evaluation

Free access should buy evidence, not vanity users.

A bounded design-partner program can test one agent workflow with fixed dates, users, actions, environments, metrics, feedback duties, and stop criteria. Live high-risk dependence remains blocked until the candidate and operating controls earn it.

Candidate program

  • Synthetic or lower-risk actions first
  • Named caller and enforcement point
  • Signature, substitution, unknown-action, and bypass tests
  • Weekly comprehension and operating review
  • No automatic paid conversion
