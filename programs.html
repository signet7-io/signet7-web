What Signet7 offers

Verifying email is free. Signing the email you send is the paid part.

Anyone can check an email they received, at no cost and with no account. What an organization pays for is the other direction: signing the email your own staff send, and running Signet7 desktop on the handful of work addresses that move money, jobs, or clients — purchasing, billing, payroll, legal. Each of those emails gets its own listing. You do not buy a seat for every employee.

What we offer

Three parts, and only one of them is paid

Signing the email you send

Signet7 signs the email you have already written, so anyone receiving it can prove the wording is genuine. You turn it on yourself, and you keep writing in your own mail application.

Checking email you receive — free

No account and nothing to install. Anyone can check the email asking them to move money, change a payment, or release a record. One person or a thousand use the same page. We compare the wording against the signature; we do not keep a readable copy of your mail.

Signet7 desktop — a few work addresses

An app on an office computer that already opens those mailboxes. It checks incoming email on the addresses you name, and can sign what you send. Ordinary email passes without comment; you only hear from it when a signature does not hold.

Cost today: nothing. Use is free while Signet7 is in preview, and no card can be charged. See what is live today.

How this differs from a spam filter

Filters sort your mail. Signet7 is the last look before you act.

You almost certainly already pay for spam and malware filtering. Signet7 is not more of that, and it is not priced that way. A filter guesses whether a message is unwanted. Signet7 answers a narrower question with certainty: is this the wording the sender actually signed?
